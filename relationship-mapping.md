# Contact and stakeholder matching
Inputs: optional candidate-provided LinkedIn CSV (fields vary), manually provided contact list. Use only supplied and publicly accessible data; avoid unauthorized LinkedIn scraping. Normalize Unicode, name, employer, company aliases, dates, title and URLs. Direct connection status is asserted only if export establishes it; employer may be stale. Match statuses: confirmed_current_company (current public corroboration), exported_company_unverified, possible_identity_match, no_match. Identity collisions require uncertainty; never merge solely by name. Publicly identified hiring managers/recruiters/executives are separate unconnected_stakeholder records. Provide contact's relevant function, plausible outreach context, evidence link, and privacy-aware display. Never infer willingness to refer or an actual second-degree path. If export missing, report direct-contact coverage Unknown. Do not send outreach without explicit approval.
