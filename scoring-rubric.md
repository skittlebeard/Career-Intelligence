# Scoring rubric v1.0
## Eligibility
Hard constraints are separate gates (e.g. candidate-declared minimum compensation, mandatory authorization, required licensure). If unknown, mark requires_confirmation, not rejected. Only verified_open roles enter final ranking.
## Weighted relevance (0–100)
R role/capability 40%: demonstrated core requirements (0–40), comparable responsibility and seniority (0–25), relevant accomplishments/outcomes (0–20), credible transferable stretch (0–15).
C company/initiative 25%: documented technology/initiative alignment (0–40), current strategic relevance and recency (0–30), role connection to initiatives (0–30).
N relationship accessibility 15%: confirmed relevant direct contacts (0–50), public relevant hiring stakeholder identified (0–20), plausible evidenced route to introduction (0–30). Public stakeholder alone is not a relationship. No LinkedIn input does not imply zero: mark unknown components.
P career/practical alignment 20%: role direction and progression (0–35), location/work arrangement (0–25), compensation when published (0–25), travel/other expressed preferences (0–15). Unknown compensation remains unknown, not zero.
Score S = 0.40R + 0.25C + 0.15N + 0.20P when all known.
For missing dimensions, provisional_score = sum(weight_i * score_i for known i)/sum(weight_i for known i), with weights as fractions. coverage = sum(weight_i for known i). If any subdimension unknown, do not silently award points: use explicitly observed component coverage or mark the entire dimension provisional and explain. Show raw subscores, evidence links, coverage and provisional flag. Ranking policy: fully assessed scores first; provisional scores in a separately labeled section or marked non-comparable, with no false precision. Missing contact data must not disqualify roles. Candidate may elect to sort by role-fit alone.
## Evidence confidence
Separate High/Medium/Low based on primary-source strength, cross-source corroboration, recency, and ambiguity. High: current primary job source plus strong recent original initiative evidence. Medium: primary job plus some older or indirect initiative evidence. Low: primary job but weak initiative or relationship context. Confidence does not change relevance score.
## Calibration
Example A: R90 C80 N60 P85 -> 36+20+9+17=82.
Example B: R90 C80 N unknown P85 -> provisional 73/0.85=85.88, coverage 85%; NOT comparable to A's complete 82. Flag missing N and display both as distinct categories.
Balanced career strategy: reward credible transfer and advancement, but no claim of required experience without proof. A stretch role is acceptable when hard constraints satisfied and core responsibilities plausible; flag stretch rationale.
