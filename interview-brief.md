# Interview and resume brief protocol
For each top opportunity: (1) exact job and verified link/time, (2) concise company context and technology timeline, (3) what public evidence says was solved, (4) current investments and possible unresolved issues, (5) candidate-to-requirement matrix with evidence, (6) confirmed contacts vs public stakeholders, (7) 3–5 hypothesis-driven interview questions, (8) resume positioning: emphasize, clarify, cannot claim, (9) source log. Hypotheses must have alternative explanations and questions to test them. Avoid treating vendor promotional claims as independently proven results. Do not rewrite resume unless requested; then keep every factual claim traceable to the authoritative source resume or candidate confirmation.
